Clean Link: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Summary

Clean Link collects no data. It makes no network calls. It reads the clipboard only when you tap "Paste".

Data

  • The app does not collect, store or share personal data.
  • The app has no analytics, no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases.
  • The app does not have the internet permission. Links never leave your device.

Clipboard

  • The app reads the clipboard only when you tap "Paste" on the home screen.
  • The app writes the clean link to the clipboard when you share a link to Clean Link, or when you tap "Copy".

Trackers rules

The app removes trackers with the ClearURLs rules. The rules are in the app. The app does not download them.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new policy and the new date.
